## Deviations from the plan, and why

1. **Finishing (vignette and grain) runs in the deliverable encodes, not per segment.**
   - Grain at the segments' CRF 8 made x264 the bottleneck (2.5× slower), and the vignette alone cost about 30%.
   - `vs render` writes a clean review master; `-d …` produces finished deliverables.
2. **Grain is opt-in (`--grain`).** Per-frame temporal noise made the 45 s master 768 MB, against 19 MB without it. **Open decision** (see below).
3. **G2P uses espeak through kokoro-onnx, with brand-dictionary splicing, instead of misaki.** `misaki[en]` pulls in spacy and transformers. Espeak plus the dictionary reproduces the prototype exactly ("Lura" = lˈuːɹə; 1.16 s for "Meet LuraPlus.").
4. **Segment slices are 2 s, not 6 s.** An edited scene then spreads across all workers. Each slice costs one page load, about 1 s.
5. **Per-scene backgrounds instead of one global background layer.** Push and iris transitions carry the scene's own background, and segments depend only on their own scenes, which the cache needs.

## Findings worth keeping

- **Engine-animated elements must not be centered with CSS `transform`.** The engine writes the inline transform. Layout lint can't see it, because it measures before the first seek, so the golden stress fixture caught it instead. The rule is now noted in `scenes/styles.css`.
- **Prototype bug:** Karplus-Strong pitch correction should use `SR/(P+0.5)`. REFERENCE §6.2 is updated.
- **Inter-sample peaks ran 0.7 dB over sample peak** on the real mix. The limiter now detects peaks on the 4×-oversampled signal.
- **`will-change` on animated elements makes cross-order determinism much worse** (up to 123/255): composited text rasterizes at history-dependent scales. Don't add it.

## Open decisions

1. **Cross-order determinism bound.** Measured 3/255 on a few hundred channels of text edges (button and caption text after their animations). This comes from Chromium compositing history. The segment guarantee the cache relies on is exact. Proposal: amend PLAN §14 to "≤ 4/255 per channel and SSIM ≥ 0.999", or drop the cross-order test in favour of the segment test.
2. **Grain.** Choose one:
   - (a) no grain;
   - (b) static grain (cheap, but it looks like a dirty screen);
   - (c) a coarser half-resolution grain overlay like the prototype's, still to be built and measured;
   - (d) temporal grain only on `web` and `hero`, where the bitrate is capped anyway.
3. **VP9 deliverables** take 30 s each per 45 s. `-cpu-used 4` would roughly halve that at a small quality cost.
4. **Template screenshots and B-roll** are generated mock-ups (`templates/feature-launch/_source`). Swap them for real product shots when you have them.
